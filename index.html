The Designer

Crafting Stories in Silk & Gold

Kumud Boutique began as a single sketchbook of dreams — silhouettes inspired by lotus petals, heirloom textiles, and the quiet strength of women who wear their heritage with grace. Today, the atelier is a haven for those who seek couture beyond trend: garments built to be heirlooms.

Every piece begins with hand-drawn concept art, moves through artisanal embroidery, and ends on a fitting table where perfection is non-negotiable.

Artisanal

Hand-finished detailing on every garment.
